⚽ STRK · EPASS

Elite Strikers Turf Club

Dublin sports club running pitch bookings and the Elite Premier League.

  • StrikeCoin (ERC-20): prepaid credits for pitch bookings and match fees; prize money paid out in STRK.
  • Elite Pass (ERC-721): one non-transferable NFT per seasonal member, carrying their booking discount.
Open club portal
🏍 APXP · APEXG

Apex Auto & Trail Mechanics

Garage specialising in touring motorcycle prep and premium riding gear.

  • Apex Loyalty Points (ERC-20): points earned on every service, redeemed for discounts.
  • Apex Authentic Gear (ERC-721): an authenticity certificate for each helmet or jacket, transferable on resale.
Open garage portal
Test network only. No real money is used. Tokens have no monetary value.
